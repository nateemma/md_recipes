# Berbere

The backbone of Ethiopian cooking — hot, but with a long list of warm spices behind the chilli that keeps it from being merely hot. Fenugreek and ajwain are what make it taste specifically Ethiopian rather than generically spicy.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
- 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
- 1 teaspoon ajwain or caraway seeds
- 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
- 6 green cardamom pods
- 4 whole cloves
- 1 teaspoon allspice berries
- 1/2 cup sweet paprika
- 2 tablespoons cayenne, or to taste
- 1 tablespoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 tablespoon coarse salt

## Instructions

1. Toast the whole spices in a dry pan over medium heat until fragrant, 3 to 4 minutes. Fenugreek turns bitter if it darkens — watch it.
2. Cool completely, then grind to a fine powder.
3. Stir in the paprika, cayenne, ginger, cinnamon, nutmeg and salt.
4. Store airtight away from light.

## Notes

- Adjust the cayenne to taste; a traditional berbere is considerably hotter than this.
- Used in doro wat and misir wat, and good rubbed on lamb before roasting.

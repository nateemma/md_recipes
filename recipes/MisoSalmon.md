# Broiled Miso Salmon

## Ingredients

- 1 pound wild salmon, cut into 4 equal portions
- 1/4 cup white miso, available at amazon, Asian/gourmet markets
- 1/2 cup mirin
- 1/2 cup sake (optional)
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- 1 scallion, green parts only, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine miso, mirin, sake (if using) sugar, garlic and ginger
2. put the fish in a ziploc bag and add the marinade
3. Place in refrigerator and allow to marinade at least 6 hours or up to overnight
4. Remove from refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking. Preheat oven to 400F
5. Transfer fish to a sheet pan lined with foil. Discard excess marinade
6. Place fish on the second rack from the top and bake 6-7 minutes
7. Turn the oven to broil, put the fish on the top rack and broil 2-3 minutes, or until fish is slightly browned and flakes easily with a fork
8. If you wish to serve the fish without the skin, carefully slide a fish spatula between the skin and flesh
9. Top with scallions (and/or sesame seeds) and serve

## Notes

- You can do this with any slightly oily, flaky fish. It is really good with grouper or black cod, and would probably work with regular cod, bass, halibut etc.
- Don't skip putting foil on the sheet pan, or you will be trying to scrape off burnt marinade for quite a while
- This works well with coconut (cauliflower) rice and a green vegetable

# Crispy Oven Baked Chicken

## Ingredients

- 4-6 chicken thighs (enough for 4 people)
- 3 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour (plain)
- 3/4 cup cornflour (corn starch)

## Instructions

1. Trim the fat off the chicken thighs (optional).
2. In a mixing bowl, add all the spices, mix well and set aside.
3. On your chicken pieces, sprinkle approx. 2 teaspoons of the spice mix and coat each side evenly.
4. In a marinating container or mixing bowl, add the buttermilk, eggs, salt, mix well, and add bay leaves.
5. Add the spice coated chicken to the buttermilk, cover, refrigerate and marinate overnight or a minimum of 2 hours up to 24.
6. Preheat your oven to 400°F / 200°C.
7. Add your leftover spice mix to a mixing bowl along with the flour, cornflour, and baking powder. Mix well.
8. Remove the chicken pieces from the buttermilk and place in spicy flour coating one at a time. Note: only (lightly) dredge each side once - if you add too much flour, you will end up with a sticky mess. You only want a thin coating
9. Place the chicken pieces on a sheet pan covered with foil or parchment paper.
10. Leave the chicken for 5 minutes. You should see the dry flour become wet from the buttermilk marinade. If there are any dry spots, brush them with buttermilk (or leftover marinade)
11. Bake for approx 30-45 minutes at 400°F / 200°C, after 5 minutes, turn oven down to 350°F / 180°C. Optionally, flip the chicken after about 20 minutes

## Notes

- This recipe is without any oil or fat, making it a healthier option for fried chicken. The cooking time may vary depending on the size of the chicken. Use a meat thermometer to ensure the chicken is fully cooked.
- You could probably make this in an air fryer

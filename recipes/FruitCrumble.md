# Lori's Fruit Crumble

## Ingredients

Crumble Topping:

- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 3/4 cup oats
- 1 stick butter, melted
  
Fruit Filling:

- 4 nectarines
- 2 mangoes
- 1 pint raspberries
- 3 Tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 Tbsp flour

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375 °F
2. Peel and cut up the fruit and put it into a bowl
3. Add the lime juice and toss (this stops any browning/oxidation)
4. Add the flour and sugar and combine
5. Pour the fruit filling into a baking dish
6. Combine the topping ingredients in a bowl and mix together. You don't want it too smooth, it's good to have some clumps
7. Sprinkle the topping over the fruit filling
8. Put the baking dish onto a sheet pan (in case it bubbles over) and put into the oven
9. Bake until the liquid from the fruit is bubbling and the top is golden brown, about 50 minutes
10. Let it rest for at least 10 minutes before serving. This allows the filling to firm up a little
11. Serve with ice cream

## Notes

- You can half the amount of topping if you like (but obviously it will be thinner)
- Replace the fruit with whatever you have available
- If Phil is eating this, cut the cinnamon in half!

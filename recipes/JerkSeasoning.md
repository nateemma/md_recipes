# Jerk Seasoning

Allspice — pimento, in Jamaica — and Scotch bonnet are non-negotiable; everything else is adjustable. Made as a wet rub it clings and penetrates far better than a dry one.

Gloves for the chillies. Scotch bonnets are serious and the oil stays on your hands for hours.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons ground allspice
- 1 tablespoon dried thyme (or 3 tablespoons fresh)
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 to 4 Scotch bonnet chillies, stemmed
- 6 scallions, roughly chopped
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2-inch piece ginger, peeled
- 2 tablespoons dark brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons lime juice
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon coarse salt

## Instructions

1. Wearing gloves, stem the Scotch bonnets. Leave the seeds in for full heat, remove them for less.
2. Combine everything in a blender and blend to a coarse paste, scraping down as needed.
3. Rub thoroughly into chicken or pork and marinate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
4. Grill over indirect heat, ideally with pimento wood or a handful of soaked wood chips.

## Notes

- Keeps 2 weeks refrigerated, or freezes in portions.
- Filed under American cuisine because the vocabulary has no Caribbean entry — see the note in the README.

# 4 Kinds of Soft & Chewy Cookies From One Dough!

## Ingredients

Adapted from https://pastryliving.com/4-kinds-of-soft-chewy-cookies-from-one-dough/

Base dough:

- 8 oz Unsalted butter (2 US sticks)
- 3.5 oz Granulated sugar (1/2 US cup)
- 5.6 oz Light brown sugar (3/4 US cup)
- 3.5 oz Egg (2 medium-large eggs)
- 1 tsp Vanilla extract
- 13.2 oz All-purpose flour (3 US cups * Calculated as 1 cup = 125 g)
- 1 1/2 tsp Baking soda
- 1/2 tsp Salt

Chocolate Chip Cookies:

- 8.5 oz The base dough (1/4 of the whole batch)
- 2 tsp Oil
- 3.5 oz Dark chocolate chips
- a tiny sprinkle on each cookie Sea salt (Optional)

Double Chocolate Cookies:

- 8.5 oz The base dough (1/4 of the whole batch)
- 1 tsp Oil
- 1 Tbsp Cocoa powder
- 1 Tbsp Milk
- 3.5 oz Dark chocolate chips
- Some Dark chocolate to dip the cookies (Optional)

Red Velvet Cookies:

- 8.5 oz The base dough (1/4 of the whole batch)
- 1 tsp Lemon juice
- 2 tsp Cocoa powder
- 1/4 tsp Red coloring (Gel or Paste)
- Some White chocolate to dip the cookies (Optional)

Ginger Molasses Cookies:

- 8.5 oz The base dough (1/4 of the whole batch)
- 0.9 oz Molasses (1.5 Tbsp)
- 1 tsp Ginger
- 1/2 tsp Cinnamon
- 0.35 oz All-purpose flour (4 tsp)
- Some Granulated sugar to coat the dough

## Instructions

Base Dough:

- In the bowl, combine the dry ingredients.
- You can either sift them or mix them well with a whisk. And set it aside.
- Next, in a bowl, add the soft butter, granulated sugar, and light brown sugar.
- Mix until they are well combined.
- To this, add vanilla and eggs and mix. The egg will look separated in the beginning but continue mixing until the batter looks smoother and well combined.
- Finally, add the dry ingredients and mix until they are evenly incorporated.Divide into four to make the four flavors.

Chocolate Chip Cookies:

- In the base dough (1/4 of the whole base dough), add oil and chocolate chips. Mix until they're well combined.
- Divide the dough into 2 oz (60 grams or about 4 tablespoons) and form a ball shape.
- Sprinkle a tiny bit of sea salt on top before baking. This is totally optional, you can omit it if you like!
  
Double Chocolate Cookies:

- To make the double chocolate cookies, add oil, milk, cocoa powder, and chocolate chips to the base dough. And again, mix until well combined.
- Divide the dough into 2 oz (60 grams or about 4 tablespoons) and form a ball shape.
- This double chocolate dough does not spread so much in the oven,
- unlike the other three ones - Push the surface by hand before baking it until it spreads to about 2.25-2.5 inches.

Red Velvet Cookies:

- Add lemon juice, cocoa powder, and red coloring to the base dough. And mix!
- Divide the dough into 2 oz (60 grams or about 4 tablespoons) and form a ball shape.
  
Ginger Molasses Cookies:

- Add molasses, ginger, cinnamon, and all-purpose flour. And mix!
- Chill them in the fridge completely until they get firm - they are too soft to form a ball.
- Tip:You can also place the dough in the freezer to speed up the chilling process when you are in a rush.
- Divide the dough into 2 oz (60 grams or about 4 tablespoons) and form a ball shape.
- For the molasses cookie dough, coat sugar around the dough before baking it.
- By applying a tiny amount of water, the sugar sticks more to the dough. You can omit water if you prefer less sugar on the cookies.
  
Baking the cookies:

- Bake (Preheated) at 350 F | 176 C for 11 - 13 mins. Adjust the time and temperature accordingly.
- It's important not to overbake the cookies to enjoy the soft and chewy texture. The cookies feel too soft when they are hot but wait until it cools down - It'll get firmer and perfectly soft. 11 to 12 minutes is the perfect baking time with my oven but this can vary, depending on the oven. Please adjust accordingly.
- The doughs spread a bit more in the oven when they are left at room temperature. You don't need to wait and bake them right away in the preheated oven with this recipe.

## Notes

  - With this recipe, using cold eggs is not going to fail the result but I recommend leaving them at room temperature whenever you have time just so that they combine a little bit more easily.
  - Scrape off the side and bottom of the bowl and paddle after adding eggs and flour to ensure the batter is evenly blended.

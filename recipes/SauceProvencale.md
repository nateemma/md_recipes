# Sauce Provençale

Sauce tomate pushed south: garlic, white wine, olives and a lot of herbs. Good with fish, lamb or eggs, and better the day after it is made.

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 3 cups Sauce Tomate (see Sauce Tomate)
- 1/2 cup black olives, pitted and halved
- 1 teaspoon chopped thyme
- 1 teaspoon chopped oregano
- 2 tablespoons chopped basil
- Kosher salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook gently for 2 minutes, until fragrant but not coloured.
2. Add the white wine and reduce by half.
3. Add the sauce tomate, thyme and oregano, and simmer gently for 20 minutes.
4. Stir in the olives and warm through for 5 minutes.
5. Off the heat, add the basil. Season with salt and pepper.

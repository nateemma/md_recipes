# Sauce Moutarde

Hollandaise with Dijon whisked in at the end. Simple, and the sharpness cuts the butter in a way that suits oily fish — mackerel, herring, grilled salmon.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Hollandaise (see Hollandaise)
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- Kosher salt and cayenne

## Instructions

1. Have the hollandaise warm and off the heat.
2. Whisk in the mustard a spoonful at a time, tasting as you go.
3. Sharpen with lemon juice and season with salt and cayenne.
4. Serve warm. Do not reheat over direct heat once the mustard is in.

## Notes

- Wholegrain mustard gives texture but a less smooth sauce; use half and half if you want both.

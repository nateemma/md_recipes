# Key Lime Pie

This is a really good and really easy pie

## Ingredients

- 1 pre-made graham cracker crust
- 4 tsp grated zest from 3-4 limes
- Juice from the (same) 3-4 limes
- 3 large egg yolks
- 1 14 oz can sweetened condensed milk
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1/3 cup cream cheese
- Whipped cream (canned or homemade) to top

## Instructions

1. Whisk egg yolks and lime zest together in a medium bowl with a hand mixer until tinted light green, about 2 minutes
2. Beat in the cream cheese, sour cream and sweetened condensed milk, then the lime juice. Set aside to thicken for about 20 minutes
3. Pre-heat oven to 325F
4. Pour the lime filling into the crust
5. Bake on middle rack until center is set but still wiggly when jiggled, 15-17 minutes
6. Move pie to wire rack and cool to room temperature
7. Refrigerate until well-chilled, about 3 hours
8. Cut into slices, top with whipped cream and serve

## Notes

- Zest the limes before juicing them
- Do not use Evaporated Milk. This recipe works because the acid in the limes reacts with the starches in the sweetened condensed milk to help thicken the filling
- This works with lemons too

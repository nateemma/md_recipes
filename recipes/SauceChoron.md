# Sauce Choron

Béarnaise with concentrated tomato folded in, which turns it a pale coral and rounds the sharpness. Very good with grilled beef or with eggs.

The tomato must be thick and dry, or it thins the emulsion and splits it.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Sauce Béarnaise (see Sauce Béarnaise)
- 3 tablespoons tomato purée, or 2 tomatoes cooked down to a thick paste
- Kosher salt and cayenne

## Instructions

1. If using fresh tomatoes, peel, seed and chop them, then cook over medium heat until reduced to a thick dry paste, about 10 minutes. Cool to lukewarm.
2. Fold the tomato into the béarnaise a spoonful at a time.
3. Season with salt and cayenne. Serve warm, not hot.

## Notes

- Cold tomato will seize the butter. Lukewarm is the aim.

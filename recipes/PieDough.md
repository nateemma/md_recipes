# The Best Pie Dough

Adapted from:
<https://pastryliving.com/the-best-pie-dough-for-a-flaky-pie-crust-with-video/>

## Ingredients

- 13.2 oz All-purpose flour (3 US cups *Calculated as 1 cup = 125 g*)
- 1 Tbsp Granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp Salt
- 8 oz Unsalted butter (2 US sticks)
- 3.9 oz Cold water (1/3 + 1/8 US cups)
- 1 Tbsp Oil (I use olive oil. Any regular oil works.)

## Instructions

1. Add flour, sugar, salt, and cold cubed butter into a food processor and pulse them together until the butter gets small pieces like seeds of apples or whole black peppers.
2. Move it to a bowl, add water and oil and mix until it forms a ball
3. Rub the surface, flip, and continue until the liquids go around evenly.
4. Press the surface well, flip, and repeat it until it comes together. Stop when it is still slightly powdery but can form a ball by hand.
5. Let it rest in the fridge at least 1 hour or ideally for more than a few hours to rest the dough before you roll it.
6. Cut the dough in half, wrap them individually, and roll them to make a circle or desirable shape.
7. Pinch the edge and smooth it out to prevent it from cracking too much as you roll the dough.
8. Chill in the fridge for one hour or ideally for more than a few hours to rest the dough before you roll it.

## Notes

- Apply bread flour frequently on the way so the dough does not stick to the parchment paper and a rolling pin.
- When you roll the dough, add the same pressures from both hands to make evenly spread dough.
- I recommend rolling it very thin (0.1 inch | 2-3 mm) to maximize the amazing crispiness of the crust.
- The butter in the dough gets softened quickly when you roll the dough in a warm environment. Work on it in a cooling space and move as fast as possible.
- Chill the dough whenever you feel it is getting too sticky to handle.
- Chill the dough for more than a few hours before baking. It prevents  shrinkage in the oven and also creates a better texture.
- If you are using a particular recipe, follow the baking time and temperature.
- Wrap tightly and store it in a fridge for up to 5 days or in the freezer for a few months.

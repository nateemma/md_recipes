# Rough Puff Pastry

The butter goes in as cubes rather than a block, and the rolling and folding does the laminating as you go. Four turns instead of six, an hour and a half instead of a day, and perhaps eighty per cent of the rise.

For a tarte tatin, a sausage roll or a pie lid, nobody will ever know the difference.

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 pound (4 sticks) cold unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 3/4 cup ice cold water
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Toss the flour, salt and cold butter cubes together in a bowl. Do not rub the butter in — you want the cubes intact and coated.
2. Add the water and lemon juice and stir with a knife until it comes together into a shaggy, lumpy mass. It should look wrong.
3. Tip onto a floured surface and pat into a rough rectangle. Roll out to about 8 by 20 inches. The butter will streak; that is exactly right.
4. Fold in three like a letter, give it a quarter turn, and roll out again.
5. Repeat for four turns in total, chilling 20 minutes after the second if the butter softens.
6. Wrap and chill at least 30 minutes before using.

## Notes

- Cold hands, cold water, cold butter. Work near an open window in summer.
- The lemon juice relaxes the gluten slightly and makes rolling easier.

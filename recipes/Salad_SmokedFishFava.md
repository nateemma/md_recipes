# Smoked herring tartare with fava beans & lime

This recipe from Amélie Darvas at
Hai Kai is "tartare" like you've never had it before-she starts with smoked fish and pairs it with a swoosh of crème fraîche sparked with Meyer lemon. The smoke, herbs, cream, and acidity (Meyer lemon juice and lime juice) produce waves of flavor.

I like to add arugula to make a more substantial salad, maybe some croutons for crunch

## Ingredients

- Coarse sea salt
- 8 ounces (250 g) fava beans in the pod (see Note), shelled (½ cup/125 g)
- 2 tablespoons crème fraîche or sour cream
- ½ tablespoon fresh Meyer lemon juice
- Fine sea salt
- 8 ounces (250 g) smoked fish, such as herring or blue fish, skinned and cut into ¼-inch (6-mm) pieces
- 2 tablespoons micro or baby basil leaves
- 2 tablespoons micro or baby amaranth or spinach leaves
- 2 tablespoons herb oil
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- Freshly ground pepper
- Piment d'Espelette (or any mild chilli flakes)

## Instructions

1. Fill a small bowl with half ice, half cold water.
2. In a small saucepan of boiling salted water, blanch the fava beans for 1 minute.
3. Drain the favas and add to the ice water to cool for 1 minute.
4. Drain again, then peel them.
5. In a small bowl, whisk the crème fraîche with the lemon juice.
6. Season with fine salt.
7. In a medium bowl, combine the favas with the smoked fish, basil, amaranth, herb oil, and lime juice and gently toss to coat.
8. Season with fine salt, pepper, and piment d'Espelette.
9. Spoon the tartare in a line down the center of each plate.
10. Spoon the lemon cream next to the tartare and serve.


## Notes

- You can skip the blanching and peeling of the fava beans in Step 1 if you use 3 ounces (90 g) thawed frozen baby peas (about ½ cup/125 ml).
- The peeled favas and the lemon cream can be refrigerated separately overnight.
- if you don't have herb oil, just make a vinaigrette using lime juice instead of vinegar
- add some finelay grated lemon zest to the crème fraîche

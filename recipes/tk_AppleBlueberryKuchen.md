# Thomas Keller's Blueberry and Apple Kuchen

This is from Keller's book, *The French Laundry*. This is one of the few rustic dishes in the book.
The original recipe uses cranberries, but I much prefer blueberries

## Ingredients

Kuchen:

- 6 tbsp or 3 oz unsalted butter, at room temperature, plus butter for the 9-inch cake pan
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/2 cup milk
- 3 medium Golden Delicious apples, thinly sliced (1/4 inch wedges)
- 1 cup cranberries or blueberries
- Cinnamon sugar: 1 tbsp sugar mixed with 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon (or lavender, or cardamom)

Hot Cream Sauce:

- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 8 tablespoons or 4 oz unsalted butter

## Instructions

Kuchen

1. Preheat oven to 350F
2. Beat butter, sugar, and egg together in a mixer until the mixture is fluffy.
3. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, and nutmeg in a big bowl
4. Add the dry ingredients and milk alternatively to the mixer, and mix until all the ingredients are evenly combined.
5. Mix the 1 tbsp sugar with your coice of cinnamon/lavender/cardamom and leave to infuse until needed.
6. If using lavender leavers/flowers, remeber to remove before using!
7. Cut the apples into very thin slices.
8. I might not have produced the slices with the thickness desired in the book, but I think I did an okay job.
9. Insert the apple slices into the batter (ends pointing towards the center so they kind of look like spokes on a wheel)
10. Spread the blueberries/cranberries over the top of the cake
11. Sprinkle the cinnamon/lavender/cardamom sugar all over the top of the cake
12. Bake for 35-40 minutes
13. Remove from the oven and leave to cool for 10 minutes
14. Remove from the pan and put on a plate

Hot Cream Sauce:

1. Put the sugar, heavy cream and room-temperature butter into a small saucepan and heated it over low heat until the butter had melted
2. Crank up the heat a bit to bring it to a boil
3. After it has come to a boil, turn down the heat to a simmer for about 8 minutes, until it has thickened

Serving:

1. When ready to serve, cut a slice and top with the cream sauce or ice cream

## Notes

- The original recipe uses cranberries instead of blueberries
- I'm not a big cinnamon fan, so I might try this with lavender (put leaves flowers into sugar for a while before using), or maybe even cardamom
- This is probably good with pears too
- If you don't want to make the cream, just use vanilla ice cream instead - it won't suck

# Chinese Five-Spice

Built to cover the five flavours, and star anise dominates by design. Sichuan peppercorns are what make it tingle; without them it is a sweeter, flatter blend.

Use it sparingly — it is assertive and easily takes over a dish.

## Ingredients

- 8 whole star anise
- 2 tablespoons Sichuan peppercorns
- 2 tablespoons fennel seeds
- 2 cinnamon sticks (cassia if you have it), broken
- 2 teaspoons whole cloves

## Instructions

1. Toast all the spices in a dry pan over medium heat, shaking, until fragrant, about 3 minutes.
2. Cool completely.
3. Grind to a fine powder — star anise is woody, so you may need to sieve and regrind the coarse bits.
4. Store airtight.

## Notes

- Sichuan peppercorns should be sifted of their black seeds, which are gritty and add nothing.
- Good in a marinade for pork belly or duck, or mixed with salt as a dip.

# Sauce Portugaise

Sauce tomate with a great deal of fresh tomato concassé stirred through, so it keeps some texture and brightness. Classically served with, or stuffed into, whatever vegetable is in season.

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 pounds ripe tomatoes, peeled, seeded and diced
- 2 cups Sauce Tomate (see Sauce Tomate)
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 2 tablespoons chopped flat-leaf parsley
- Kosher salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 8 minutes, until soft.
2. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
3. Add the diced tomatoes and cook over medium-high heat for 10 minutes, until much of their liquid has gone.
4. Stir in the sauce tomate and simmer gently for 20 minutes.
5. Season with sugar, salt and pepper, and stir in the parsley off the heat.

## Notes

- To peel tomatoes: score a cross in the base, 15 seconds in boiling water, then into ice water. The skins slip off.

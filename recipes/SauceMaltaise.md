# Sauce Maltaise

Hollandaise made with blood orange juice and zest in place of some of the lemon. Classically served with asparagus, and worth making for the six weeks a year blood oranges are about.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Hollandaise (see Hollandaise)
- 3 tablespoons blood orange juice, reduced to 1 tablespoon
- 1 teaspoon finely grated blood orange zest
- Kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Reduce the blood orange juice in a small pan until syrupy and about a tablespoon remains. Cool to lukewarm.
2. Whisk the reduced juice and the zest into the hollandaise.
3. Season with salt and serve warm over asparagus.

## Notes

- Reducing the juice matters: added raw it thins the sauce and tastes watery.
- An ordinary orange with a squeeze of lemon stands in outside the season.

# Café de Paris Butter

From a Geneva restaurant that has guarded the recipe since the 1940s, so every version is a reconstruction. The list is absurd and the result is worth it — anchovy, capers, mustard, brandy and a dozen herbs, all disappearing into butter.

Made a day ahead it is much better. The flavours need time to find each other.

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces (2 sticks) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 4 anchovy fillets, mashed
- 2 tablespoons capers, rinsed and chopped
- 2 shallots, very finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tablespoons brandy
- 1 tablespoon chopped tarragon
- 1 tablespoon chopped chives
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 1 teaspoon chopped dill
- 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground white pepper
- A pinch of cayenne
- Juice of 1/2 lemon

## Instructions

1. Combine everything except the butter in a bowl and mix well. Cover and leave at room temperature for 2 hours, or refrigerate overnight — this is where the flavour develops.
2. Beat the softened butter until creamy.
3. Beat the marinated mixture into the butter until evenly combined.
4. Roll in parchment into a cylinder and chill until firm.
5. Slice into thick coins and melt over grilled steak.

## Notes

- Better on the second day. Make it ahead.
- Also good stirred into a pan of just-cooked green beans, or melted over roast chicken.

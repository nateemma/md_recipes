# Easy Chicken Kebabs

Adapted from: https://cooktoria.com/easy-chicken-kebabs/

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. boneless chicken thighs, cut into medium pieces
- 1/8 cup soy sauce
- 1/8 cup olive oil
- 1 tsp. dried oregano
- 3 medium garlic cloves (minced)
- 1/2 medium lemon, juiced
- 3 medium multicolored bell peppers, cut into medium pieces
- skewers

## Instructions

1. marinate the chicken
2. Place the chicken pieces in the large bowl and add the rest of the ingredients. Mix everything well. Cover, and refrigerate for 2 to 24 hours (the longer, the better).
3. arrange onto skewers
4. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat. Arrange the chicken and peppers onto the skewers.
5. grill the kebabs
6. Oil the grilling grate. Cook for about 7 minutes on each side, until the chicken is well done.

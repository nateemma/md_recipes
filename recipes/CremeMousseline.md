# Crème Mousseline

Pastry cream beaten with a great deal of soft butter until it is light and firm enough to hold its shape. This is what goes inside a fraisier or a Paris-Brest.

Both must be at the same temperature — properly room temperature, not one cold and one soft. Get that wrong and it curdles, which looks fatal and is not; see the notes.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups Crème Pâtissière (see Crème Pâtissière), at room temperature
- 10 ounces (2 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Take both the pastry cream and the butter out of the fridge at least an hour ahead. They must feel the same temperature to your finger.
2. Beat the butter in a stand mixer with the paddle until pale, light and creamy, about 5 minutes.
3. Whisk the pastry cream smooth in a separate bowl.
4. Add the pastry cream to the butter a large spoonful at a time, beating well after each. Add the vanilla.
5. Beat for 2 to 3 minutes more, until light and glossy. Use immediately, or refrigerate and re-beat before piping.

## Notes

- If it splits or looks grainy, warm the outside of the bowl briefly with a hot cloth and keep beating. It almost always comes back.
- Half the butter gives a lighter cream, sometimes called crème légère.

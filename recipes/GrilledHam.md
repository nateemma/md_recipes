# Grilled Ham with Jack Daniel's Mop

## Ingredients

For the Ham:

- 1 bone-in ham, 12 to 14 pounds (notthe pre-cooked spiral-cut kind)
- 2 Tbsp crushed black peppercorns
- 2 Tbsp crushed mustard seeds or powdered mustard
- 1 Tbsp crushed coriander seeds or powdered coriander

For the Mop:

- 1 cup Jack Daniel's whiskey (or bourbon)
- 1 cup firmly packed light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup Dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup dark corn syrup

## Instructions

1. Allow the ham to stand at room temperature for about 1 hour before grilling
2. Start grill and set it up for low heat, indirect grilling. If using a pellet grill, set to lowest setting
3. Place ham in a heavy-gauge foil baking pan
4. Score the ham across the skin in a diamond pattern by making shallow, diagonal cuts about 1" apart across the top surface
5. Rub the spices into the ham, making sure some get into the cuts you just made
6. Grill the ham over indirect low heat until the internal temperature reaches 140°F, 3 to 4 hours. On a Traeger, maybe turn the heat up to the 2nd lowest setting after an hour or two
7. After putting the ham on the grill, whisk together the mop ingredients in a medium bowl
8. Once the internal temp reaches about 130°F (about 30 minutes before the end of cooking), baste the ham with the mop every 5-10 minutes. You can also do this earlier if you like to get a more syrupy, thick crust on the ham (I do)
9. Remove the ham from the grill and allow to rest for 15 minutes
10. Slice and enjoy

## Notes

- You can score the ham and coat with dry rub the day before
- To do this properly, you need to use a pellet grill or something that can maintain a low, indirect heat
- I like to cook this an extra hour or two, but start the first 1-2 hours on the "smoke" setting of my Traeger
- I prefer bourbon to Jack Daniel's
- If you don't have dark corn syrup, just mix light corn syrup and molasses (3:1)
